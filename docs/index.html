What it brings

Not add-on features — these are Pilaster's own capabilities that Explorer is missing.

Dual-pane view

Two independent panels — each with its own history, syncable navigation, and drag-and-drop copy/move between them.

Colorful tags

macOS-style tags in any color and name on any file or folder — filter by them with one click from the sidebar.

Favorites

One click on the heart icon and anything is reachable from the sidebar — with a faint marker for missing targets and one-click removal.

Customizable quick access

Pin your own folders by right-click or drag, and reorder them however you like — everything is saved, even after a restart.

Its own copy indicator

Not Explorer's green bar: real control — pause, resume, cancel, speed and time remaining, multiple operations at once.

Color themes

Light, dark, or system-following, with a selectable accent color — or automatically follow your own Windows accent color.
